## Long description (EN)

Downtify downloads music to your box. Paste a Spotify, YouTube Music, Deezer or SoundCloud link, or search by artist, album or song, and you get an audio file with title, artist, album, track number and cover art already filled in. Metadata comes from Spotify's public pages and audio from YouTube Music, so no Spotify account, API key or Premium subscription is needed.

Tracks are saved to Music/Downtify in your Documents, where any music player on the box can read them. You can pick another folder at install time.

Downtify can also watch a playlist and download new tracks as they are added, import a CSV exported from Soundiiz, TuneMyMusic or Exportify, and play your library in the browser.

You sign in with your Lazycat account, and box administrators get admin rights in Downtify.

## Long description (ZH)

Downtify 把音乐下载到你的微服。粘贴 Spotify、YouTube Music、Deezer 或 SoundCloud 链接，或按歌手、专辑、歌曲搜索，即可得到已填好标题、歌手、专辑、曲目编号和封面的音频文件。元数据来自 Spotify 公开页面，音频来自 YouTube Music，因此无需 Spotify 账号、API 密钥或 Premium 订阅。

歌曲保存在你文档中的 Music/Downtify，微服上的任何音乐播放器都能读取。安装时也可以指定其他文件夹。

Downtify 还可以监控歌单并自动下载新增歌曲，导入 Soundiiz、TuneMyMusic 或 Exportify 导出的 CSV，并在浏览器中播放你的音乐库。

使用懒猫账号登录，微服管理员即 Downtify 管理员。
